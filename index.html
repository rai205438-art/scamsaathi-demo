2. Quick safety rules

  • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, password or screen-sharing access.
  • You generally do not need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Do not call numbers or use links supplied by a suspicious caller; verify through the official app or website.
  • Do not delete messages or transaction records that may help an investigation.
  • If money has been lost, contact your bank/payment provider immediately and report it promptly.
If money has been lost in India: call 1930 as soon as possible and use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. Do not delay while using this demo.
